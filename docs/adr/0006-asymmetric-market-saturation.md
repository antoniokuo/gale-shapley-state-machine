# 6. Asymmetric Bipartite Market Saturation Model

Date: 2026-08-05

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The primary empirical objective is to measure human cognitive processing latency ($T_{cognitive}$) during cascading algorithmic state transitions. A standard symmetric 1:1 matching matrix resolves with high efficiency and minimal displacement chaining, failing to generate the cognitive friction required for valid NASA-TLX psychometric evaluation.

## Decision

The state machine implements a Many-to-One asymmetric market structure consisting of 30 Proposers and 10 Receivers. A static capacity quota of 3 is enforced upon all Receivers, resulting in a perfectly saturated bipartite market (30 total available holds).

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Mathematical guarantee of market congestion and deep cascading displacement chains, ensuring sufficient algorithmic complexity for cognitive load testing.
- **Positive:** Accurately mirrors real-world enterprise matching constraints (e.g., Hospital-Residents problem, server load-balancing).
- **Negative:** Increased layout density requires strict spatial management in the DOM to prevent visual clutter from skewing the usability (SUS) telemetry.
